GUIDED WALKTHROUGH

Explore the app, one screen at a time.

Scroll through six screens showing the app's key features. Images are promotional illustrations; exact interface details may change between releases.

STEP 01 / 06

Enter your headings

Set your aircraft heading and target heading, then select Left, Right or Shortest. Compass Turns prepares the training correction for the selected region.

  • Enter headings directly
  • Use the target heading slider
  • Choose the intended turn direction
STEP 02 / 06

Understand your turn summary

Review direction, ONUS correction, estimated rollout indication, heading change and rate-one timing. The explanatory section shows how the numbers fit together.

  • Check rollout indication and correction
  • See estimated turn time
  • Read the training explanation
STEP 03 / 06

Watch the compass turn

Open Visualize to follow an animated magnetic compass with changing indications. Simulate, pause and reset while reviewing live training values.

  • Start and pause the animation
  • Observe compass indications
  • Follow changing heading values
STEP 04 / 06

Select a training region

Select a supported training region based on the hemisphere and teaching conventions you are studying.

  • Open Training region
  • Choose the relevant preset
  • Return to Calculation
STEP 05 / 06

Customize your correction table

Enter your instructor-approved reference correction magnitudes and OS/US types. Custom values remain saved on your device.

  • Adjust correction magnitudes
  • Choose overshoot or undershoot
  • Save for future practice
STEP 06 / 06

Choose your settings

Switch your appearance, select a region, review app updates and access help or legal information.

  • Light, Dark and System themes
  • Updates and version history
  • Feedback and support
Training use only. Compass Turns is an educational training aid, not an approved flight-navigation instrument. Confirm procedures and corrections using your instructor and approved training references.
